These rules are in effect for all new uploads on the site. Failure to abide by them may lead to staff relinquishing your upload rights and in certain cases, for example when involving malware, a ban.
Included below are also our naming rules for each section, this is in regards to the name on the site and not specifically the name of the files. Name of the files can vary depending on your source, and in general they should not deviate from the original source.
If you rip your own content, try to follow these as closely as possible. If you re-upload, generally you should keep the original format. Further details to be found on this page.
These rules are subject to change at any time, new uploads after rule changes may be postponed to be fixed for a little bit. We will try to advertise rule changes.
- 1.0 Staff has final say in any torrent moderation, changes that needs to be made or if a torrent is allowed to be uploaded or not. Staff interprets the rules at their discretion.
- 1.1 Torrents not meeting the rules laid out in this page will get deleted.
- 1.2 Multiple infractions and excessive wrongdoing will have your upload rights revoked for a period or permanently.
- 1.3 If you do not have a trusted uploader rank, all torrents must be approved by staff before they go live on the site.
- 1.4 All uploads need to retain their original release name. No so called "RETAGS". If you have done transformative work on a title, in which case credit to original ripper, encoder or uploader is a must.
  - 1.4.1 Just removing some subtitles or an audio track does not count as transformative work and will get deleted if spotted.
  - 1.4.2 Adding something new to the file, like new subtitles counts as transformative work. Must still give credit to original source.
  - 1.4.3 If we find that the uploaded torrent has been changed from its original release by some other site before it's uploaded, it might still get deleted. Torrents from sites like IPT, TD, TL etc often have changes made to them. Be vigilant.
- 1.5 NFO is for the most part optional. If the release comes with an NFO, you may use it on the site but it is not required to be kept in the torrent folder.
  - 1.5.1 Do not make a new NFO or add an NFO to a torrent that does not have it from the start. Do not add new files at all to someone elses torrent, unless making transformative work, see 1.4.
- 1.6 No advertising other trackers. Automatic censorship will be added to the uploaded NFO/Details. Trying to circumvent this will cause action to be taken against the uploading account.
- 1.7 The .torrent-file uploaded cannot exceed 1MiB in size.
- 1.8 No special lettering in the name on the site, except periods, underscores or dashes (., _, -).
- 1.9 Scene releases that can be found in a PRE-DB must be unpacked, rar-files removed, SAMPLE folder, NFO-file removed. But NFO should be uploaded on site to give credit. NFO-file can remain with app and game torrents if it contains installation instructions.
  - 1.9.1 If you are unsure if it's a scene release or p2p release, check [predb.net](https://predb.net/) or [predb.org](https://predb.org/) for the release.
- 1.10 Tags are mandatory, if no tag, use on of the following:
  - -NOGROUP
  - -NOGRP
  - -NOTAG
  - -P2P
- 1.11 If a torrent is to be tagged NORDIC it must include subtitles from SE, NO, DK, FI.
- 1.12 Dupes are not allowed. Staff can sometimes at their own discretion constitute what goes as dupe in a case by case scenario. Internal groups have priority on uploaded content, and will be allowed to keep a dupe up, in case another release was uploaded before the internals.
  - 1.12.1 Another type of rip, encode or source for music, movies and tv is not considered a dupe. x264 and x265 content can live side by side, as can a 1080p and 720p, disc and web-dl etc.
  - 1.12.2 See 8. Trumping for when dupes are no longer allowed.
- 1.13 No custom, non-official or AI-upscaled content is allowed, neither are machine/AI-translated subs.
- 1.14 Excessive useless files in the torrent will probably get it deleted.
- 1.15 All upload of visual media must include MediaInfo and preferably, include a couple of screenshots. Screenshots are not a requirement, but it is nice to have.
- 1.16 All uploads must properly reflect what they contain in the name, see some of the naming rules below.
