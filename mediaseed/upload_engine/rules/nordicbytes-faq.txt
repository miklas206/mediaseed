If your ratio is above 1, you are contributing to the community. Is it below 1, it means you are draining the community. And if it's below 0.4, your download rights will be revoked.

Freeleech is when a torrent is free to download, meaning no download will be registered. Please keep in mind that the seeding rules still apply to freeleech torrents (1:1 or 48 hours).
All newly added torrents will automatically be marked ‘Green’ for 24 hours. This means they are freeleech, but only half upload will be counted in that period. When the 24 hours are over, you will receive full upload again, but they will also cost normal download again. The uploader of the torrent will be receiving full upload.
Every day at 4PM, 5% of the platform’s torrents are selected in a lottery, where the chosen torrents are granted freeleech and double upload for 24 hours! (Only torrents with at least 1 seeder and that are not featured are included in the pool). When the clock strikes, the discounts on the old torrents expire, and the new ones take effect. At the same time, the most popular GREEN torrent is selected and given ‘Featured’ status on the front page for 7 days, which grants freeleech and double upload during that period.
A torrent community is all about sharing, to encourage seeding back to the community, then you will earn points (shards) when you seed. The shards you earn can be used in the store to buy different goods and perks like Upload, Download, Invites and so on. You will only earn shards for a torrent AFTER you have seeded the mandatory required 48 hours.
The formula for shards is not public knowledge, but it is based on the following criterias.
- How large the torrent is
- How long you have seeded that specific torrent
- How many others are seeding on the same torrent
- If you are connectable or not
